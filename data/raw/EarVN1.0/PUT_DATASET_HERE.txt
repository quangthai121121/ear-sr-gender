Giai nen bo du lieu EarVN1.0 vao day, sao cho cac folder subject nam
TRUC TIEP duoi thu muc nay (khong can thu muc "images/" trung gian), vi du:

  data/raw/EarVN1.0/001.ALI_HD/*.jpg       (subject 1, nam)
  data/raw/EarVN1.0/002.LeDuong_BL/*.jpg   (subject 2, nam)
  ...
  data/raw/EarVN1.0/098.<ten>/*.jpg        (subject nam cuoi cung)
  data/raw/EarVN1.0/099.<ten>/*.jpg        (subject nu dau tien)
  ...
  data/raw/EarVN1.0/164.<ten>/*.jpg        (subject cuoi cung)

Ten folder co the la "01", "001_", "001.ALI_HD"... deu duoc -- code
(src/data/build_metadata.py) tu dong doc SO DAU TIEN trong ten folder de
xac dinh subject_id, phan con lai (".ALI_HD", "_"...) khong quan trong.

Neu ban co thu muc "images/" trung gian (data_root/images/<subject>/...),
code cung tu dong nhan dien duoc, khong can sua gi.

Neu de dataset o day, KHONG can sua configs/paths.yaml (da tro san vao
thu muc nay theo mac dinh). Neu de o noi khac, sua paths.yaml ->
paths.data_root cho khop.

File nay chi la placeholder de giu lai thu muc rong trong zip -- xoa di
sau khi da giai nen dataset that vao day.
